Projects

My recent work asks whether AI systems are grounded in what they are given: an audit of whether medical vision-language models actually read the X-ray, an LLM agent that answers questions over a live data warehouse, and a threat model for LLMs that make decisions inside city infrastructure. Behind them is a track record of published research on intrusion detection, Wi-Fi sensing and compact vision models, and of distributed systems built to survive failure.

Research

  • Research · Multimodal AI

    Auditing visual grounding in medical VLMs

    Controlled image interventions show which chest X-ray report models actually read the image, and which write a convincing report without looking. Preregistered, in progress.

    View case study

  • Research · IoT security

    Drift-resilient IoMT intrusion detection

    Adaptive Hoeffding trees that update on the fly as medical IoT traffic shifts, instead of retraining from scratch. First-author paper under review.

    View case study

  • Research · Efficient models

    Lightweight plant-disease recognition

    A controlled benchmark of phone-sized models for crop-disease recognition. The proposed model was the best overall and ran faster than the other Ghost-based models.

    View case study

  • Research · Wireless sensing

    CSI-based fall detection

    Device-free fall detection from Wi-Fi signals with competing convolutional kernels. Published in IEEE Internet of Things Magazine, 2025.

    View case study

  • Research · LLM security

    Behavioral security for LLM-integrated smart cities

    A threat model for cities that let LLMs act on sensor and vehicle data, traced across three layers of city operations, with a proposed framework of defenses.

    View case study

  • Research · Computer vision

    Flood detection from drone imagery

    Transfer learning with ResNet, InceptionNet and Xception to recognize flooding in drone photos.
