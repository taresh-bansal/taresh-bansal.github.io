Lightweight plant-disease recognition

Mar – Aug 2026 · BITS Pilani

Efficient models · Computer vision

Which small models are worth deploying for crop-disease detection?

A controlled comparison of 9 models under 2.2M parameters across 3 datasets (118k images, up to 115 diseases), aimed at models small enough for phones.

  • 9Models compared
  • 118kImages
  • 69.8%115-class accuracy
  • 10.5 msPer image

Question

Many small image models claim to be mobile-ready. Which ones are actually worth deploying for crop-disease detection, once they are compared on the same data and measured the same way?

Approach

Compared 9 models under 2.2M parameters on 3 datasets, including the proposed 2.03M-parameter model and several Ghost-based models (GhostNet-style lightweight convolutions). Tested CBAM, a common attention add-on, as an ablation, and timed inference per image, averaged over 200 runs.

Results

  • The proposed 2.03M-parameter model was best overall, with top accuracy on 2 of 3 datasets.
  • On the hardest, 115-class dataset it led the next model by 3.8 points (69.8% against 66.0%).
  • CBAM attention hurt rather than helped, costing 3.4 points of accuracy on the hardest dataset.
  • It took 10.5 ms per image (200-run average), 40% faster than the other Ghost-based models (17–18 ms).
