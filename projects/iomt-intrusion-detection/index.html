Drift-resilient IoMT intrusion detection

Jan – Feb 2026 · First-author paper, under review

Medical device security · Learning on the fly

An attack detector that keeps learning as hospital network traffic changes

Hospital devices send patient data over networks that attackers can reach. Attacks change over time, so a detector trained once goes out of date, and retraining it from scratch is slow. This work builds a detector that updates itself with every new record.

Aim

Build an attack detector for networks of medical devices that stays accurate as attacks change, without stopping to retrain.

What was done

  1. Use real hospital-style data. A public testbed (WUSTL-EHMS-2020) that records both network traffic and patients' vital signs, each record marked normal or attack.
  2. Learn from a stream. Records arrive one at a time and update a decision tree on the spot, so there is never a slow retraining step.
  3. Recheck old decisions. The tree regularly revisits its earlier splits, replacing ones that are no longer best and pruning branches that no longer help.
  4. Compare fairly. Against four models that also learn as they go and three that must be retrained from scratch, on accuracy and on time per update.

What was found

  • Most accurate of every model tested, both those that learn as they go and those retrained from scratch.
  • No false alarms on the test set: every record it flagged as an attack was an attack.
  • Fast enough to keep up. Each update takes less time than retraining any of the batch models, so the detector never falls behind the traffic.

Why it matters

Hospitals cannot take a security system offline every time attackers change tactics. A detector that learns continuously, and corrects its own past decisions, stays useful without that downtime.

Publication

Taresh Bansal, Siva Sai, Vinay Chamola, Mohan Gurusamy. “Drift-Resilient Intrusion Detection in IoMT Networks Using Adaptive Hoeffding Trees with Internal Node Reassessment.” Under review at IEEE Internet of Things Magazine. Publication entry
