Research

My papers apply machine learning to IoT: drift-resilient intrusion detection for medical IoT networks, and device-free fall detection from Wi-Fi signals. One is published in IEEE Internet of Things Magazine (2025) and one is under review there.

2026

  1. IoT Security
    IEEE Internet of Things MagazineUnder review

    Drift-Resilient Intrusion Detection in IoMT Networks Using Adaptive Hoeffding Trees with Internal Node Reassessment

    Taresh Bansal, Siva Sai, Vinay Chamola, Mohan Gurusamy

    IEEE Internet of Things Magazine, Under review

    An incremental intrusion detector for Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) network traffic. Adaptive Hoeffding trees with internal node reassessment let the model adapt as the traffic distribution shifts, instead of being retrained from scratch: each update takes 0.65 s, against 41.5 s to retrain NuSVC (a support-vector classifier) and 199 s for Isolation Forest. On the WUSTL-EHMS-2020 dataset it reaches 99.94% accuracy.

  1. Wireless Sensing
    IEEE Internet of Things Magazine2025

    A Novel Competing Convolutional Kernels Method to CSI-Based Fall Detection for Disabled People

    Siva Sai, Taresh Bansal, Vinay Chamola

    IEEE Internet of Things Magazine, 2025

    DOI

    Detects falls from Wi-Fi channel state information (CSI), a non-wearable alternative for disabled and elderly people. Time series are classified with randomly generated convolutional kernels grouped into competing pattern dictionaries, using hard and soft counting and first-order differences. On a public dataset the method reports 98% accuracy and 89% F1, outperforming the prior state of the art by about 9%.
